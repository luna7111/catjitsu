Privacy Policy
CatJitsu Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

CatJitsu stores only the information necessary to provide the service, including:

username;
email address;
encrypted password;
avatar selection;
language preference;
accessibility preferences;
audio volume preferences.

Gameplay information such as current matches may also be temporarily stored while games are in progress.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

authenticate users;
personalize the application;
manage multiplayer matches;
save user preferences.
3. Password Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are securely hashed using Django's authentication system.

4. Cookies

CatJitsu may use authentication cookies or session tokens required for login. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

5. Third-Party Services

CatJitsu does not sell personal information to third parties.

If external authentication or third-party services are added in the future, this policy will be updated.

6. Data Retention

Account information is retained while the account exists.

Users may request account deletion, after which personal information will be removed where technically possible.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are taken to protect user information, but no Internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the project maintainers.
